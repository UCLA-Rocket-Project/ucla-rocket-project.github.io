ARES
FLAGSHIP


Project Ares is our flagship liquid bi-propellant
rocket team using Ethanol and LOX. With 50+ static
fires and 9 launches, Ares is the most prolific
liquid rocket program in the world, setting many
world records along the way.

Learn More

PROMETHEUS
HYBRID


Project Prometheus is our introductory nitrous
hybrid rocket. Designed and built entirely by first-
years in under 20 weeks, Prometheus is the only
program of its kind, giving first-years real ownership
and the entire engineering design process.

Learn More

ENGINEERING
1RK (E1RK)


Engineering 1RK: Intro to Rocket Design
is a 2-unit course taught by Rocket Project
undergrads, where students get to design
and launch their own solid rocket in a single
quarter. Gain experience with Solidworks,
OpenRocket, and much more.
